# Open-hypothesis frontier after T82 — the ontology may not be supplied

Date: 2026-08-02  
Status: **POST-CLOSURE ADMISSION CONTRACT; NO METHOD OR RUN ADMITTED**

## 1. Localized missing capability

T75 proved that active evidence selection can reduce a complete reliable
acquisition phase by `86.875%`. T82 proved the algebra needed to accumulate
likelihood evidence under adaptive action, then closed as a research method:
once every candidate mechanism is supplied, the architecture is a standard
differentiable finite Bayes or particle filter.

The surviving model-level gap is therefore not posterior arithmetic. It is:

> construct and revise the candidate explanatory space itself from raw partial
> observations and interventions, while knowing when the current space does not
> contain an adequate explanation.

This is the knowledge-formation branch of T79. A better filter cannot recover a
true mechanism outside its support, and a stronger policy cannot act on a
variable the representation never formed.

## 2. Required open-world operator

A future candidate must implement one bounded update

\[
(\mathcal H_t,u_t,D_t)
\longmapsto
(\mathcal H_{t+1},u_{t+1},D_{t+1}),
\]

where:

- `H_t` is a versioned set of executable or behaviorally testable hypotheses;
- `u_t` is explicit probability or evidence mass for `unknown / none of the
  above`; and
- `D_t` records dependencies, provenance, protected behavior, and description
  cost.

The operator must learn when to:

1. **create** a hypothesis not present in the initial library;
2. **split** one state or mechanism whose predictions hide consequentially
   different futures;
3. **merge/compress** redundant hypotheses without losing decision-relevant
   distinctions;
4. **reject or retire** a hypothesis using fresh evidence rather than prose
   plausibility; and
5. **revise locally** after a change while preserving unaffected predictions.

T82's exact filter may be used only after this operator proposes a testable
candidate. The filter is a control/backend, not the new method.

## 3. Information and impossibility boundary

No method can guarantee creation of a useful hypothesis absent all three of:

- a proposal language or generative support containing an adequate model;
- observations/interventions whose outcome laws distinguish it from existing
  alternatives; and
- enough search, interaction, and state budget to locate and retain it.

The proposal language is an inductive bias and a charged resource. A universal
program language moves the problem into search time; a narrow DSL moves it into
coverage failures. Pretrained semantic priors move cost into training data and
parameters. None is free.

An `unknown` state is necessary but not sufficient. It can detect aggregate
misspecification only when calibration is reliable; it does not identify the
missing variable or mechanism.

## 4. Shortcuts that do not satisfy the contract

- Supplying a complete candidate list, latent variable, object slot, causal
  parent set, factor boundary, or program grammar production as a truth label.
- Calling residual thresholding, a likelihood gate, or posterior expansion
  “concept invention” without producing a new executable predictor.
- Comparing against an exhaustive table, fixed-query learner, or generic RNN
  while omitting PSR/state-splitting, program induction, predicate invention,
  open-world mixture, expanding-expert, and exact-search controls.
- Accepting a new hypothesis because it compresses the training transcript
  without prospective interventional and decision-value validation.
- Claiming local revision from sparse parameter edits without protected
  functional behavior and complete dependency tests.

## 5. Evidence required before a method earns code

A proposed formation operator must provide, in this order:

1. an explainable constructive rule that maps a witnessed predictive failure
   to a bounded candidate search distribution;
2. a coverage theorem or an explicit proposal-probability lower bound for a
   nontrivial family, including search cost;
3. a sound admission/retirement test under adaptive data and repeated proposals;
4. a complete description/state/interaction/compute ledger against the
   strongest matched formation control; and
5. an order-one witness where candidate formation—not supplied filtering or
   planning—changes end-to-end behavior.

Only then may a CPU falsifier be preregistered. Mathematical correctness alone
does not earn a run if the construction is already a known algorithm under a
new name or if the hard candidate is supplied.

## 6. Model-level success boundary

The primary evaluation begins with raw observations and legal actions and ends
with decisions after transcript deletion and later change. Success requires:

- at least 20% lower complete lifetime regret or cost than the strongest
  equal-information learned and algorithmic formation control, with the
  simultaneous confidence bound clearing 20%;
- a causal ablation where removing learned create/split/merge operations removes
  the gain while leaving T82-style filtering available;
- retained unknown-mass calibration when the true mechanism is outside the
  proposal support;
- transfer to held-out mechanisms, bindings, compositions, and at least one
  natural tool/scientific/reasoning stream;
- no protected prior-capability loss above one absolute point; and
- full accounting of proposals, rejected candidates, verifier interactions,
  persistent bytes, execution, search, training, and serving work.

Twenty percent is the acceptance floor, not the design target. Results from
`10%` to less than `20%` are arguable but do not satisfy the goal; they earn at
most one bounded replication when the evidence can plausibly move the frozen
confidence bound across `20%`. A point estimate at or above `20%` does not pass
when that bound remains below `20%`. Every single-digit result closes the
candidate as the main lane. A synthetic pass is a formation-component result;
a smarter-model claim requires the same frozen operator to improve an open
model on a natural interactive domain.

## 7. Direct current-art boundary

Open-world model expansion is already an active systems direction.

- [Hypothesis-driven Model Expansion under
  Uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06501) generates, verifies, and updates
  abstract state/transition hypotheses during robot planning using foundation
  models.
- [Dynamic predicate
  invention](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jJbzfjODQt) constructs and repairs
  symbolic causal world models online with meta-interpretive learning.
- [LODGE](https://openreview.net/forum?id=gkf81Ciu9K) uses LLM proposals,
  hierarchy, simulation, and environment grounding to generate predicates,
  operators, preconditions, and effects.
- [WALL-E](https://openreview.net/forum?id=DorAT49sxj) extracts executable
  symbolic environment knowledge from exploration trajectories and reports
  large open-world agent gains.
- [Mechanistic World
  Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12474) identifies reusable explanatory
  mechanism organization as the broader autonomous-discovery target.

These works prevent a novelty claim for “LLM proposes, environment verifies,”
predicate invention, symbolic model repair, or hypothesis-aware planning. They
also expose the remaining seam: most systems pay for foundation-model priors,
basic predicates, object/state features, simulators, legal skill libraries, or
task-specific symbolic grammars. A new candidate must state which of those
interfaces it removes, how it replaces the lost information, and why the
replacement beats the strongest system receiving the same raw observations and
actions.

## 8. Current decision

No open-hypothesis mechanism is selected. The next research action is outward
mathematical search for a constructive proposal rule with non-negligible
coverage and cheaper verification than generation. Do not reopen T82, T77
state splitting, CEGAR, PSR/Hankel discovery, Lie/holonomy structure, or generic
program search without a new strict advantage over their strongest matched
controls. No CPU, local GPU, or rental GPU is admitted by this contract.
