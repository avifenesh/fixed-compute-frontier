# Independent audit — T68 holonomy-triggered state birth

Date: 2026-08-02  
Audited file: `results/holonomy-triggered-state-birth-t68.md`  
Scope: exact mathematics, named prior-art collisions, and admission as a mechanism for substantial intelligence gains. No experiment was run.

## Verdict

**CLOSE AS AN INTELLIGENCE MECHANISM; RETAIN ONLY A REVISED ALGEBRAIC DIAGNOSTIC; NO RUN.**

T68.1 and the orbit-reachability part of T68.2 are correct after two convention fixes. T68.3 is correct only as a finite causal-state/deterministic-sufficient-statistic cardinality result; as written, it overreaches toward generic predictive representations and bits. The proposed synthesis remains somewhat distinct—use loop obstruction to propose and retract a shared group factor—but no acquisition advantage survives a matched group-factorized CSCG/HMM/PSR/recurrent control. Current work already occupies holonomy diagnostics, holonomy-induced representation dimension, sequential de-aliasing, state splitting, predictive-state discovery, and long-memory recurrent world models. There is no theorem or pre-run argument connecting this synthesis to substantial general intelligence improvement.

## Exact mathematics

| Item | Audit | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| Path convention | **Correct.** For traversal `p=e_1...e_m` with a left action, `g(p)=g_{e_m}...g_{e_1}` gives `f\mapsto g(p)f`; `g(pq)=g(q)g(p)` and `g(p^{-1})=g(p)^{-1}`. The induced map on based loops is therefore an anti-homomorphism, which does not affect identity tests. | State the concatenation rule explicitly; it is what makes the noncommutative proof auditable. |
| Gauge statement | **Correct.** Under vertex-frame changes `a_v`, `g_{u\to v}` transforms as `a_v g_{u\to v}a_u^{-1}`, so a loop based at `r` is conjugated by `a_r`. | None beyond giving the formula. |
| T68.1 obstruction theorem | **Correct.** The forward product telescopes in the displayed order. Conversely, the loop `p_u e p_v^{-1}` has product `h_v^{-1}g_eh_u`; identity gives `g_e=h_vh_u^{-1}`. No commutativity is used. | Specify that `Q` is finite and that `|E|` counts **unoriented geometric edges**. If `E` contains both orientations, the stated `\beta=|E|-|V|+1` is wrong. |
| Fundamental-cycle sufficiency | **Correct with a basepoint convention.** Rooted chord loops `p_u e p_v^{-1}` form a free basis of the graph fundamental group. If their holonomies are identity, every word in them is identity even for noncommutative `G`. | Say “rooted fundamental loops,” not merely that each chord creates a loop. These `\beta` constraints are an algebraic basis, not statistically independent evidence: they share tree-edge estimates, and another cycle basis can have very different noise conditioning. |
| T68.2 lift | **Correct.** Reversible labels make `(u,f)\mapsto(v,g_ef)` a deterministic lifted transition. `K_r` is a subgroup despite the anti-homomorphism convention, and returns from `(r,f_0)` reach exactly `K_rf_0`. | A nonidentity element of `G` can lie in the action kernel or stabilize `f_0`; it then births no state. The diagnostic relevant to representation is the induced action on the reachable orbit—not raw `g(ell)\ne I`. Orbit elements correspond to the coset set `K_r/\operatorname{Stab}_{K_r}(f_0)`; this is not generally a quotient group because the stabilizer need not be normal. |
| Lift size/compression | **Correct but loose.** The full lift has `|V||F|` states. From one start, every visible vertex has a transported copy of the root orbit, so the reachable component has `|V||K_rf_0|` states when finite. | “History is compressed to an orbit element” is conditional on a supplied/identified group action and a finite orbit. T68 provides neither factor invention nor an identification theorem. |
| T68.3 predictive lower bound | **Conditionally correct, presently overstated.** Pairwise future-distinguishable reachable fibers yield at least `|K_rf_0|` equivalence classes of histories under predictive (causal-state/Myhill–Nerode) equivalence. | Require a **finite** orbit and, for each pair, one common legal intervention/test whose outcome laws differ. Restrict the conclusion to a deterministic exact sufficient statistic of history, or explicitly to the number of causal-state values. It is not a lower bound on HMM nominal latent-state count, PSR/OOM linear dimension, or neural width: a lower-dimensional belief or predictive vector can assume many distinct values. The `ceil(log2 |K_rf_0|)` claim applies only to losslessly encoding one of finitely many discrete predictive-state labels; it is not a generic finite-precision memory bound. For an infinite orbit, state only that no finite discrete exact representation exists under the pairwise condition. |
| Noise hypothesis | **Conceptually right, not yet a test.** Fresh-loop validation and accounting for loop selection are necessary. | `g_e=h_vh_u^{-1}+noise` is meaningful only after choosing a matrix embedding and noise law; additive noise generally leaves the group. A valid null must include edge-estimation covariance, shared-edge correlations, basis selection, and multiple testing. |

Thus the exact obstruction theorem survives; the claimed semantic implication does not. Holonomy certifies failure of one flat factorization. It does not identify omitted state rather than curvature, estimator error, nonfaithful action, or a misspecified transport family.

## Prior-art collision and the surviving seam

- [Gauge-invariant representation holonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21653) already turns composed local transports around loops into a gauge-invariant learned-representation diagnostic, with estimator stability and loop-discretization analysis. Its “aliasing” experiment is CNN sampling/boundary aliasing, not POMDP observation aliasing, so it does **not** already provide state birth; the candidate overstates this particular collision. It nevertheless occupies the core diagnostic idea.
- [Holonomy Grid Codes for Generalisation Under Directed Actions](https://openreview.net/forum?id=RJBvW7ZdhG) is the closest collision: it uses twisted action representations, derives holonomy-dependent internal dimension, and claims transfer by holonomy class for prediction/planning. This occupies both “holonomy requires representation” and much of the proposed shared-factor transfer rationale. It does not supply online factor birth, but a T68 learner must now beat this structured representation, not rediscover its effect.
- [CSCG](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-22559-5) already lifts aliased observations into context-specific clones and supports uncertainty, planning, transfer, and split/merge use of preallocated capacity. [gradCSCG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12382) takes this from raw image streams and explicitly identifies dynamic clone recruitment as future work. Therefore on-demand recruitment remains open in gradCSCG, but sequential de-aliasing and the lifted-state mechanism do not.
- [Fast State Discovery for HMM Model Selection and Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v2/siddiqi07a.html) already uses state splitting driven by dynamic context and held-out likelihood. Holonomy may be a proposal heuristic, but “birth a state when context demands it” is old.
- [PSR theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4167) and the [PSR/OOM unified framework](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/v16/thon15a.html) define state by action-conditioned future predictions and can have linear dimension smaller than the number of distinct predictive states. They directly implement the behavioral quotient that T68.3 adds only as a condition. End-to-end PSR work has also demonstrated compact learned models used for planning, so orbit cardinality supplies no acquisition advantage by itself.
- Structured recurrent world models already learn compact long-memory state from raw trajectories; for example, [S4WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02064) compares recurrent, transformer, and structured-state-space backbones on long imagination, context recall, reward prediction, and memory reasoning. The user’s goal permits useful inductive bias even when a generic recurrent model can represent the solution, but the fair control must receive the **same group action/factorization**. Such a control can update the fiber recurrently without waiting to close a loop and can tie every representational benefit claimed here.

The only seam not directly occupied is the full combination: maintain a cycle basis online, use persistent loop residuals to propose the smallest shared group orbit, validate it on new interventions, then grow/merge/retract that factor. This is a plausible model-selection heuristic, not a new intelligence principle or established acquisition gain.

## Why the acquisition claim fails the gate

The suggested advantage—one shared fiber replaces many independent clones—is a property of **factorization supplied to the learner**, not of holonomy-triggered birth. A factorized CSCG, equivariant HMM, PSR with the corresponding predictive basis, or recurrent control with the same group update shares parameters in the same way. Moreover, T68 must estimate local transports and obtain closing trajectories before it can fire; the matched structured control can learn the same local update from open paths. A fundamental-cycle basis reduces redundant algebraic checks, but does not establish fewer environment interactions and can be statistically ill-conditioned because the loops share tree edges.

Before any run, admission would require an environment family and proof (or comparably sharp pre-run derivation) showing all of:

1. the finite group action and behavioral orbit are identifiable from the allowed interventions under noise;
2. online T68 reaches a sufficient state with substantially lower sample complexity, regret, or model-selection error than **group-factorized** CSCG/HMM, PSR/OOM, and recurrent controls—not merely unfactorized clones or a generic transformer;
3. the saving includes loop-closing, transport fitting, false-birth control, merging/retraction, and compute/memory costs; and
4. the result predicts large held-out gains in counterfactual prediction, planning, or reward on raw, stochastic, non-grid environments and unseen topologies.

Without that result, a grid/toroid benchmark would mostly reproduce existing holonomy and CSCG phenomena. It could validate an implementation, but could not establish a smarter production model or substantial progress toward real intelligence. The appropriate action is therefore **no run and move the intelligence frontier outward**, while preserving T68.1–T68.2 and a narrowed T68.3 as a corrected diagnostic note.
