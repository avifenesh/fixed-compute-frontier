# fixed-compute-frontier

Standing rules for this repo. The user's own words in the current conversation come first, above these rules and above any skill.

## Results bar

Do not present a sub-percent loss gain or similar incremental polish as a research breakthrough. Before paying for expensive physical validation, require a qualitative capability change, a scaling-law break or a large compute-equivalent gain. The success floor (at least 20% on a major end-to-end part of the learning process) and how to measure it are defined in `README.md`.
